# Voice samples

Put 10–60 seconds (in total) of clean voice clips here (mp3, wav, ogg or m4a, no music or background noise),
then click **Settings → Voice & sound → Create Capri's voice from her clips** to clone them with Fish Audio.

You can also use **Open the voice samples folder** in Settings (`%APPDATA%\Capri\voice-samples`); clips there
are used instead of the ones in this folder.

No clips are shipped with Capri. Only clone a voice that is yours or that you have permission to use.
Audio files in this folder are ignored by git.
